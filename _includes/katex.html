{%- comment -%} _includes/katex.html — load with: {% if page.math %}{% include katex.html %}{% endif %} Only \( \) and \[ \] are listed as delimiters. Kramdown converts every $$...$$ in your markdown into one of those two before KaTeX ever runs: \( \) when the maths sits inside a paragraph, \[ \] when it is on its own line. That is what decides inline vs display — not the number of dollar signs you typed. Deliberately NOT listed: $$ and $. Including them papers over a broken kramdown setup, and then the same source renders differently depending on config. If the maths below fails to appear at all, kramdown is not converting — fix that in _config.yml rather than adding $ back here. {%- endcomment -%}
