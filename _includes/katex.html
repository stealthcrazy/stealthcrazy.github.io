{%- comment -%} _includes/katex.html — load with: {% if page.math %}{% include katex.html %}{% endif %} Only \( \) and \[ \] are listed as delimiters. Kramdown converts every $$...$$ in your markdown into one of those two before KaTeX runs: \( \) when the maths sits inside a paragraph, \[ \] when it is alone on its line. Position decides inline vs display — not how many dollar signs you typed. Deliberately NOT listed: $$ and $. Adding them back masks a broken kramdown setup and makes the same source render differently depending on config. If inline maths shows as literal $...$, the post still has single dollars in it — fix the post, not this file. {%- endcomment -%}
